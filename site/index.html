How many boxes do I need?

Our showroom's rule: room area plus 10% for cutting and breakage (15% for diagonal laying or 600 x 1200 mm slabs), rounded up to whole boxes.

12 boxes

An estimate only. For walls, multiply wall height by the length of the walls you are tiling. Buy all the boxes for one room together, as shade can vary slightly between batches.

Pickup and delivery

  • Same-day godown pickup for in-stock tiles ordered before 4:00 pm (Monday to Saturday).

Returns and payment

  • Unopened, undamaged full boxes can be returned within 15 days with the bill.
  • Opened boxes, cut tiles and clearance designs cannot be returned.
  • Cash, UPI, card or bank transfer. Orders above Rs 50,000 need a 30% advance to book.
